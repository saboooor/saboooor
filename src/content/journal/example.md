I heard children playing in the playground as I walked past, it reminded me of my own self.

The feeling of hearing pure chaos turn into white noise reminded me of the days I used to sit alone in a corner somewhere no one else would be during recess.

I never was the type to play with groups of people, I always found more peace in being alone, but I still wanted the company of others.

It was almost always just me while everyone else was playing with each other.

To this day I still search for that one person I can be alone with amongst the chaos that turns into gentle white noise.
